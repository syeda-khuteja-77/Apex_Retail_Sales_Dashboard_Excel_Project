# 📊 Apex Retail Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 🏪 Project Overview

The **Apex Retail Sales Dashboard** is an interactive sales analysis dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel. The project focuses on analysing retail sales performance, tracking key business metrics, and presenting meaningful insights through dynamic visualisations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, quantity sold, regional performance, salesperson contributions, and payment preferences.

The goal of this project is to transform raw retail sales data into an easy-to-understand visual report that supports data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Dashboard Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Identify top-performing salespeople.
- Analyse profit contribution by product category.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Present business performance through an interactive dashboard.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights three important business metrics:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| 💰 Total Sales | 93,859 |
| 📈 Total Profit | 15,256 |
| 📦 Total Quantity Sold | 260 |

## 📊 Dashboard Visualisations

The dashboard includes five major visualisations:

**1. Sales by Region**
- Compares sales performance across East, North, South, and West regions.
- Helps identify regional sales contributions.

**2. Top 5 Salespersons**
- Displays the top five sales performers.
- Helps evaluate individual sales performance.

**3. Profit by Category**
- Analyses profit distribution across different product categories.
- Categories include Clothing, Electronics, Furniture, Home Decor, Others, and Sports.

**4. Sales Trend**
- A line chart illustrating sales movement over time.
- Helps identify fluctuations and overall sales patterns.

**5. Quantity by Payment Mode**
- Compares quantities sold through Card, Cash, and Online payment methods.
- Provides insights into customer payment preferences.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to filter the data based on:

- 🌍 **Region:** East, North, South, West
- 💳 **Payment Mode:** Card, Cash, Online
- 🛍️ **Category:** Clothing, Electronics, Furniture, Home Decor, Others, Sports

These filters make it easier to explore specific business segments and analyse performance from different perspectives.
